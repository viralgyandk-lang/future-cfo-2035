Title: Existence, Not Payback | Why Indian CFOs Retired the 90-Day AI Test | Future CFO_DP 6 Oct 2026
Description:
On 6 October 2026 the trade press still carries a June argument Indian CFOs have already had in public. Girish Goenka, CFO of Piramal Realty, told a BW Businessworld–Salesforce roundtable that the old question — how much does it return, and in how many days — has changed. It is not a matter of return. It is a matter of existence. Reported by Savi Khanna, BW CFO World, 17 June 2026.

This episode is a teaching case. Eastern Ghats Auto Components and CFO Leela Narayan are composite. Every quotation is from the public record.

Verified lines you will hear:
- Goenka, Piramal Realty: existence, not a days-of-payback number (17 June 2026).
- Sajal Gupta, Keystone Realtors: longer horizon; process AI can show results faster than pricing work.
- Ashish Jain, Lumina Datamatics: standard ROI applied too early disturbs innovation.
- Pankaj M. Latoria, Ravago India: labour arbitrage fails; test efficiency, growth, and strategy.
- Rishabh Bothra, Vedanta–Sterlite Copper: value is behavioural; working capital belongs in the test.
- Aneesh Kongot, Happilo: an agent reads order emails into SAP (1 June 2026, SAP Concur roundtable).
- Rahul Bothra, Swiggy: engineering releases roughly 70% higher than a year ago; finance analyst layer compressed.
- Nagesh Bailur, Randstad India: payroll formats for about 75,000 workers across about 2,000 clients; two weeks of work now two days. Human voice still closes.
- Praveer Surana, Atyati: hallucination risk on tax; regulation-specific work still has a way to go.
- Radhakrishna Sunku, EY GDS (90,000+ people, 9 countries): thousands of travel invoices matched to airline data and GST credits, into SAP.
- Hemlata Chaudhary, JSW Steel Coated Products: Excel judging a 2026 tool; downtime not yet priced.
- Mankiran Chowhan, Salesforce India: if AI cannot show ROI, you are still playing hobby.
- Rajesh Raghunath Prabhu, DBS Bank India, 24 June 2026: accountability ultimately rests with people.
- BW CFO World headline, 30 September 2026, carrying ACCA: 52% of financial services professionals fear AI’s impact on jobs.
- Jitin Diwan, IndiaMART, 31 December 2025: DPDP consent and 2026 penalty-testing sit on the CFO desk.

Recommendation in the case: do not sign a 90-day payback on the whole programme. Split it. Name the human who releases a ledger post.

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
