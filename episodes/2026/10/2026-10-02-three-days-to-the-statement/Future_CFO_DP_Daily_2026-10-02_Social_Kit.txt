Title: Three Days to the Statement | RBI MPC 5–7 Oct 2026 | Future CFO_DP Case
Description:
Three working days before the RBI Monetary Policy Committee meets on 5–7 October 2026, the last voted policy is still the August hold: repo 5.25%, neutral stance, FY27 GDP 6.7%, FY27 CPI 5.0%. A Reuters poll published 28 September found 35 of 61 economists expecting +25 bps to 5.50%. August CPI was cited at 4.82%.

This HBS-style teaching case asks the CFO of Malabar Metals Limited — a labelled composite, not a listed issuer — what to sign before Monday’s board: lock ₹400 crore, wait, or disclose a ₹1.78 crore sensitivity without changing conditional guidance.

Facts cited are public: RBI August 2026 resolution and 19 August minutes; Reuters poll 28 Sep 2026; Moneycontrol poll 25 Sep 2026; Economic Times coverage of Nomura, 29–30 Sep 2026. Company figures are teaching exhibits.

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
