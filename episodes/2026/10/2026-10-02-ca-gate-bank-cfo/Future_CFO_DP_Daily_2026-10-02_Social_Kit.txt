Description:
Three days before the RBI MPC of 5–7 October 2026, the durable question is not the repo rate. It is who may sit in a bank finance chair.
On 29 June 2026 HDFC Bank appointed Puneet Sharma CFO from 1 December 2026, CFO-designate from 1 September. Axis Bank released him on 31 August. The bank said he meets the RBI Commercial Banks — Governance Directions, 2025 (28 November 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/149).
Public-sector minimum: qualified chartered accountant; 15 years overseeing financial operations, preferably accounting and taxation; 10 of them in banks or FIs; 5 at senior management.
Northline Housing Finance and Meera Iyer are composite. They are not real. Classroom line: build the decade if the firm can wait; rent the signature only for what a supervisor will read; do not make a split role permanent.
AI drafts the note. Data lists who moved in June. Finance prices the funding. Accounting verifies the close. Human judgment decides who signs.
