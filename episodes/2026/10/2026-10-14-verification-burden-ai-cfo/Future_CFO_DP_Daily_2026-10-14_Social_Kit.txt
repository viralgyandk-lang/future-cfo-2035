Hook: Finance teams now spend 26% of the week checking AI. The verification burden is the new reality.

Post:
The Datarails 2026 CFO Sentiments Survey of 270 US finance leaders is out. Every respondent uses AI. Yet 96% spend at least 10% of their time verifying its outputs. The average is 26% of the working week.

Only 5% trust AI for board-ready materials without human review. 4% trust it with the month-end close. Lack of auditability is the top barrier (75%).

Data architecture decides the burden. Disconnected systems: 76% spend >25% of time on verification. Centralized data: 31%.

The future CFO does not accept AI outputs. They challenge them. They design the controls. They keep human judgment as the final decision.

AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
