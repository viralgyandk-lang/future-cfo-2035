Hook: The Governor did not say India is weak. He said strength today is not immunity tomorrow.

Post:
Sanjay Malhotra, at the Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave on 3 October, put two risks on the same page: an AI valuation cycle that has been supporting global markets, and West Asia commodity prices that still reach India through the external account.

Crisil, reported on 4 October, says a hike of up to 50 basis points is largely manageable. Median gearing of rated firms is about 0.5. Coverage stays near 5 times. That is a median. It is not your covenant.

HDFC Bank's provisional book as on 30 September: advances Rs 32.20 lakh crore, up 16.3%. Deposits Rs 33.28 lakh crore, up 18.8%. Time deposits up 22.8%. CASA up 10.8%. Growth is real. The mix is dearer. The bank also mobilised $11.5 billion under the RBI's FCNR(B) swap window.

Four numbers before you trust a model: coverage at +25 and +50 bps, gearing against the covenant, liability mix, commodity beta.
