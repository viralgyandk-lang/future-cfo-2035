Title: Today's Resilience Is Not Tomorrow's Immunity | RBI and HDFC Bank | Future CFO_DP
Description:
On 3 October 2026, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra told the Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave that today's resilience may not imply tomorrow's immunity. He did not call an imminent crisis. He named five channels: global debt, stretched AI-linked valuations, non-bank leverage, private credit, and cyber risk. A slowdown in the AI investment cycle could reprice that chain. A correction in advanced economies may redirect capital toward India.

HDFC Bank provisional figures as on 30 September 2026, Financial Express 4 October: gross advances Rs 32.20 lakh crore, up 16.3 percent; deposits Rs 33.28 lakh crore, up 18.8 percent; time deposits up 22.8 percent; CASA up 10.8 percent; 11.5 billion dollars mobilised under the RBI FCNR(B) swap window.

Sources: Ishaan Gera, Moneycontrol, 3 October 2026; Rajeev Jayaswal, Hindustan Times, 3 October 2026; Financial Express, 4 October 2026.
