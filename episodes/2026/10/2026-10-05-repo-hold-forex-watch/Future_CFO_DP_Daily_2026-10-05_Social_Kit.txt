Description:
The RBI repo rate was reported held at 5.25 per cent in early October 2026, with a neutral stance — the fourth consecutive pause on the BW CFO World telling. Borrowing costs look stable. The risk around them is not.

In this Future CFO_DP daily, Dr. Debasis Pahi walks business students through what a CFO actually takes to the board:

- Primary corridor versus weekend commentary. August 2026 resolution: repo 5.25%, SDF 5.00%, MSF and Bank Rate 5.50%. Do not quote an unverified line that puts the SDF at 5.5%.
- Named colour from Shilpa Bhatter (UGRO Capital), Vinod Francis (South Indian Bank) and Arun Poddar (Choice International): FY27 CPI around 4.6%, core around 4.4%, GDP 6.7–6.9%, reserves close to $697 billion.
- Governor Sanjay Malhotra at the Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave: “Today’s resilience may not necessarily imply tomorrow’s immunity.” West Asia commodities, global debt, AI-linked valuations, private credit, cyber risk.
- Crisil, via Business Today on 4 October 2026: a hike of up to 50 basis points is largely manageable for India Inc. A system buffer is not a covenant waiver.
- A teaching composite, Godavari Precision Components, shows why a 12% landed-cost shock on the unhedged import book can dwarf the interest hit from a wider spread.

Philosophy: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgement decides.

Post:
On 5 October 2026 the trade press is still sitting with a hold: RBI repo at 5.25%, neutral stance, described as the fourth consecutive pause.

That is the easy slide.

The slide that matters is Governor Sanjay Malhotra at the Kautilya conclave: today’s resilience may not imply tomorrow’s immunity. West Asia commodities. Orderly, but real, equity weakness (Nifty 22,421.95, −198.5, on the ET page that carried the speech). Crisil saying India Inc can absorb 50 basis points — a system sentence, not a covenant waiver.

A CFO who freezes WACC because the repo cell did not change is letting the model decide.

Three tests before Wednesday:
1. Quote the August corridor (SDF 5.00 / repo 5.25 / MSF 5.50) until the October resolution PDF is in the data room.
2. Put the unhedged import book next to the floating-rate interest line, in rupees.
3. Do not let an agent reprint August and call it a recommendation.

AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgement decides.
