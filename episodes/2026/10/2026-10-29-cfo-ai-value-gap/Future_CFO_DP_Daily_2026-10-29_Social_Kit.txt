Title: CFOs Confront the AI Value Gap | Investment Outpaces Maturity | Future CFO_DP 29 Oct 2026
Description:
Financial services are increasing AI investment faster than any other sector, yet maturity remains flat and cost governance is lagging. In this episode of Future CFO_DP, Dr. Debasis Pahi examines the latest HTEC AI Value Realization Report 2026 and Deloitte’s Q3 2026 CFO Signals Survey.

Key findings:
• 88% of FSI firms increased AI investment (42% significantly)
• Maturity stuck at 44% scaling/AI-native — same as overall market
• 78% say people & processes constrain value more than technology
• ~80% of CFOs now have a formal role in AI decisions
• 53% still review AI costs retrospectively
• Only 20% have advanced token/workload controls
• 74% take days to weeks to pause over-limit spend

Includes a Harvard Business School-style case: NorthStar Regional Bank.

Philosophy: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.

CFOs are now in the middle of AI investment decisions — but the controls have not caught up.

New data (HTEC 28 Oct 2026 + Deloitte Q3 2026 CFO Signals):

• Financial services increased AI investment more aggressively than other sectors (88% raised spend; 42% significantly vs 28% overall)
• Yet maturity is identical: 44% scaling or AI-native
• 78% say people and processes limit value more than technology
• Finance has a formal role in ~80% of AI decisions
• 53% still review costs after the fact
• Only 20% use advanced controls
• 74% need days to weeks to pause when limits are breached

The gap is not technology. It is measurement, verification, and judgment.
