Description:
The statement has not landed. This briefing was prepared on the morning of 7 October 2026, before Governor Sanjay Malhotra's 10:00 IST Monetary Policy Statement and the noon press conference.

What the public record showed at preparation:
- RBI home page still carried policy repo 5.25%, SDF 5.00%, MSF and Bank Rate 5.50%, CRR 3.00%, SLR 18.00%.
- Reuters poll, 18-28 September 2026: 35 of 61 economists expected a 25 bp hike to 5.50% — the first increase since February 2023. 29 of 53 looked for at least one further hike by December.
- August CPI 4.82% (Reuters, 14 September), from 4.45% in July. Food 5.95%. Inside the 2-6% band, third month above the 4% target.
- FBIL USD/INR 96.4348 as at 13:00 on 6 October. 6.94% GS 2036 at 7.2150%. 91-day T-bill 5.5199%.
- 5 October overnight money-market volume Rs 7,30,002.71 crore, weighted average 5.07%. VRRR Rs 2,00,050 crore at 5.24%. Net liquidity absorbed, including outstanding operations, about Rs 5.30 lakh crore. RBI scheduled another overnight VRRR for 7 October.

Teaching case (labelled composite, not a real issuer): Mahanadi Alloys. CFO Arjun Mehta. Rs 480 crore of working-capital lines reset inside 90 days. A 25 bp pass-through is Rs 1.20 crore annualised (480 crore x 0.25 percent). Three choices before ten: lock half the book, wait for the noon press conference, or send the audit committee a range and refuse to refresh street guidance.
