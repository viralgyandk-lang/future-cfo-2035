A record 133 billion dollar diaspora inflow has left Indian banks with surplus liquidity as high as 11 trillion rupees, and pushed overnight rates below the Reserve Bank's 5.25 per cent policy rate. Reserves are close to 800 billion dollars. The 10-year held at 7.18 per cent. The rupee sat near 95.90, down more than 6 per cent this year.

This Future CFO_DP briefing, dated 2 October 2026, is a teaching case for business students — not a forecast and not investment advice. Sajjid Chinoy (JPMorgan) has argued for a hike and for liquidity absorption so there is no inadvertent easing. Nomura, Deutsche Bank and ANZ are among desks calling the first hike since early 2023. A Business Standard poll says the Bank may raise the repo by 25 basis points. The Monetary Policy Committee has not spoken.

What a CFO does on Monday: separate the policy rate from the effective rate, reprice the hedge book, put 7.18 into any borrowing planned through March, stress two paths only, and write one page for the board.
