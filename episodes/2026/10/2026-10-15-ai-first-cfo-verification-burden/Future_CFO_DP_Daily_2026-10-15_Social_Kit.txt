Hook: 62% of CFOs now own AI strategy. Their companies grow 23% faster. But their teams spend 26% of the week fact-checking the same AI.

Body:
The IBM Institute for Business Value 2026 CFO Study (1,500 CFOs) is clear: organizations led by AI-first CFOs posted 23% higher revenue growth from 2022-2024. 62% of CFOs have expanded into enterprise technology or AI strategy leadership. 90% of those AI-first leaders let AI play an active role in investment and M&A decisions inside defined guardrails.

Yet the Datarails 2026 CFO Sentiments Survey of 270 finance leaders paints the operational reality: finance teams spend an average of 26% of the working week verifying or correcting AI outputs. 96% spend at least 10% of their time on this. Only 5% trust AI to produce board-ready materials without human review. Lack of auditability ranks as the top barrier (75%).

Deloitte data adds that 54% of finance leaders already lead cross-enterprise AI and technology capital allocation.

The gap between strategy ownership and operational trust is the new CFO challenge. For business students and early-career finance professionals the implication is direct: the premium will go to those who can challenge AI outputs with domain judgment, design verification workflows, and reallocate capital dynamically.
