On 7 October 2026 the RBI Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50 percent. The rate vote was unanimous. The stance moved from neutral to calibrated tightening by 4–2. Governor Sanjay Malhotra said rate cuts are off the table in the near term.

This Future CFO_DP daily walks business students through the public numbers only: SDF 5.25 percent, MSF and bank rate 5.75 percent, CPI for 2026–27 at 5.2 percent, real GDP at 7.1 percent, and the difference between a headline and an all-in borrowing cost.

- The corridor, and why 5.50 percent is not your loan rate
- Which facilities reprice, and which do not
- How to label a 50–75 bps analyst scenario so it is not mistaken for RBI guidance
- What Ind AS 23, the effective interest rate, and Ind AS 116 do not let you book off a speech

Hook: The RBI just ended the easing phase. Most students will quote 5.50%. A future CFO quotes the reset date.

On 7 October 2026 the MPC raised the repo rate 25 bps to 5.50%, unanimous. Stance: calibrated tightening, 4–2. SDF 5.25%. MSF and bank rate 5.75%. Governor Sanjay Malhotra: cuts are off the table in the near term. Next move is a hike or a pause.

Forecasts worth memorising, then leaving alone: CPI 2026–27 at 5.2% (from 5.0%). Core 4.4%. Real GDP 7.1% (up 40 bps). Q3 CPI projected at 6.0%.

Do not add 25 bps to every WACC by habit. Do not book a lease remeasurement because the Governor spoke. Ind AS 116 does not work that way. Do not call an HDFC Bank economist’s 50–75 bps view the RBI’s path.

Board line for 8 October: reprice floating debt on the actual reset, retest hurdle rates, protect cash conversion, and do not freeze investment that still earns its cost of capital.
