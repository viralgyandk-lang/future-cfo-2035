Hook: IBM data just dropped: AI-first CFOs delivered 23% higher revenue growth. Only 6% of finance teams are ready. The role is shifting from scorekeeper to operating-system architect.

Post:
The 2026 CFO Study (IBM + Oxford Economics, 1,500 CFOs) is unambiguous. Organisations led by AI-first CFOs grew revenue 23% faster than peers from 2022 to 2024. Yet only 6% of finance organisations call themselves transformation-ready.

62% of CFOs already say their role includes enterprise AI strategy. 80% of AI-first CFOs co-architect business model experiments. 84% track AI value and reallocate capital. 90% let AI participate in investment and M&A decisions inside clear guardrails.

At Anthropic, finance built 70+ custom AI functions that finish a near-complete monthly review in 30 minutes. At OpenAI, the team is building toward a zero-day close with live dashboards. In India, Swiggy’s finance team is vibe-coding, and the FICCI CFO Summit 2026 focused on reimagining the finance role.

For business students the scarce skill is no longer just modelling. It is designing the operating system: data foundations, agent workflows, controls, and the judgement that sits above them.
