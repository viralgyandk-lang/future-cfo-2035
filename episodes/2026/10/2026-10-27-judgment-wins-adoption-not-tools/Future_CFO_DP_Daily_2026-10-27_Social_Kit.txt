Body:
In October 2026 interviews and roundtables, finance leaders from Elgi Equipments, Philips India, Swiggy, Randstad, and others describe the same reality: heavy AI investment, local efficiency gains, and still-elusive enterprise returns.

Indranil Sen (Elgi) says the differentiator three years from now will not be who bought the most tools. It will be who embedded AI into everyday business decisions. Dev Tripathy (Philips) adds that great CFOs will challenge AI outputs, refine them, and turn them into action. The role becomes more relevant, not less.

For business students the implication is practical. Master data foundations. Practise constructing the strongest case against every AI output. Keep accounting verification and financial measurement before the final human judgment.
