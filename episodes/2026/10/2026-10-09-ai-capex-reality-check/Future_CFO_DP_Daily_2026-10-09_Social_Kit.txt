Hook: Nasdaq down 1% on OpenAI revenue news. Hyperscalers still spending at 116% growth. Which number should a CFO trust?

Body:
Yesterday's market reaction to OpenAI's annualized revenue figure is a clean teaching moment.

Public reports put OpenAI's run-rate roughly $20 billion short of what some investors had been led to expect. The Nasdaq Composite closed down 1.25%. Shares of Oracle, Nvidia and AMD all sold off.

At the same time, Goldman Sachs expects S&P 500 earnings to rise 27% in the just-ended quarter — the third consecutive quarter above 25% — driven largely by AI infrastructure spend. Hyperscaler capex is projected to have grown 116% year-on-year.

Two data points from the CFO community:
• Grant Thornton (early October): 80% of surveyed U.S. CFOs expect net profit growth over the next 12 months; 84% say AI ROI is meeting or exceeding expectations.
• IBM Institute for Business Value: 62% of CFOs report expanded responsibility for enterprise technology or AI strategy.

The FICCI CFO Summit 2026 in Hyderabad, themed “Reimagining the Finance Role in the Age of AI,” featured Microsoft India and Google India finance leaders stressing decision intelligence, data foundations and practical operating models.

For students preparing to become CFOs the lesson is not “AI is good” or “AI is a bubble.” It is: finance owns the measurement system that turns AI ambition into verified returns.

What unit of AI ROI will your future board accept?
