Hook: The comment window closed in July. The circular is not final. The control gap is already on your book.

On 24 June 2026 RBI published draft Guidance on Regulatory Principles for Model Risk Management (PR 2026-2027/528). It covers banks, NBFCs, co-operative banks, ARCs and credit information companies. It covers third-party models and AI/ML. It asks for a board-approved framework, an inventory, independent validation, and a way to override, suspend or deactivate a model — including a kill switch.

BW Legal World, this morning, still treats the paper as a draft. Do not wait for the final text to ask four questions: Is it on the inventory? Who validated it? Who can switch it off at 9 pm? Does the customer know a machine is in the conversation?
