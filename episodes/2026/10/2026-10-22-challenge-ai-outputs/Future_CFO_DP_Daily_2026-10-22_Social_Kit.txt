Great CFOs don’t accept AI outputs. They challenge them.

Today’s Future CFO_DP episode is grounded in the 22 October 2026 BW CFO World interview with Philips India CFO Dev Tripathy and the latest Salesforce and Deloitte research.

Key facts:
• 90% of CFOs using AI report positive ROI (Salesforce, Oct 2026)
• Nearly 75% say their role expanded this year — mainly because of AI
• Half are now primary decision-makers on company AI strategy
• 43% of finance leaders prioritise embedding AI to automate operations through FY2027 (Deloitte)

By 2030 the operational work will be largely automated. The differentiator is judgment, ethics, and the willingness to stress-test every forecast against context the model cannot yet see.

For business students: build AI fluency and the muscle to challenge the output.

AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
