=== LINKEDIN ===
Hook: Deloitte’s September 2026 Finance Trends 2027 report shows the CFO mandate is expanding in measurable ways.
Post:
54% of finance leaders already lead cross-enterprise AI and technology capital allocation.
48% oversee AI trust (reliable, accurate, explainable outputs) and AI/tech cost controls.
60% expect AI costs and complexity to rise substantially through 2027 and will need more sophisticated cost management.
95% are comfortable with agentic workflows in some activities — yet only 14% support full autonomy for critical decisions.

Human judgment remains the final gate.
AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.

What does this mean for business students preparing for the AI-powered economy? Measure AI costs with the same rigour as headcount. Practice defining where human review must stay when agents handle structured work.
