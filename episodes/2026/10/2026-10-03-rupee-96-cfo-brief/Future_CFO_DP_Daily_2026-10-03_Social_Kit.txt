Hook: The rupee printed 96.3. GST printed ₹2.04 lakh crore. A future CFO does not pick a favourite.

Post:
Markets were shut yesterday for Gandhi Jayanti. Monday is the first chance to test the weekend story.

Three numbers from the 2 October briefs:
• Rupee touched 96.3150, about 0.5% weaker, a two-month low, on FPI selling and higher US yields.
• September gross GST: ₹2.04 lakh crore, +14.7%. Gross is not net. National stocking is not your buyer's sell-through.
• FPIs sold about ₹36,000 crore of equities in September. UPI still did 24.07 billion transactions, +23%.

People news is counterparty news. RBI cleared Anup Bagchi as HDFC Bank MD & CEO for three years. Sidharatha Mishra takes ICICI Prudential Life for five. If your limit sits with the bank, open the sanction. Do not reprice off an ADR move.

From 1 October, service exporters — freelancers included — face monthly EDF filing. Invoice, credit advice, form, GST row.
