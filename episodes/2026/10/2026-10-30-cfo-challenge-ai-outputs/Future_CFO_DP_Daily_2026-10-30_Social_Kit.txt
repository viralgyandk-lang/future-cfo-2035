Great CFOs don’t accept AI outputs. They challenge them.

Late October 2026 data:
• Deloitte Germany: only 17% see positive AI value today; 70% expect it in 2 years; only 11% can link costs to value
• Deloitte NA: finance has a formal AI role at ~80% of firms, but most use basic controls and take days (not minutes) to respond to cost overruns
• Datarails: only 3% actively cutting headcount; 60% redeploying; 40% spend >25% of day verifying AI outputs
• Meta: AI flux analysis cut close from 7→6 days; “earn the right” before greater autonomy; 550 accountants in AI fluency training

The gap between productivity and cash-flow or decision quality remains real. Your edge as a future finance leader is knowing when to accept the draft, when to challenge the driver, and when to demand better measurement.
