Post:
By early October 2026 Oracle, Broadcom, SpaceX and peers had priced nearly $500 billion of new debt to fund AI chips, data centres and power. The BIS warns the financing is increasingly debt and private credit—opaque and interconnected. Circular arrangements link chip makers, hyperscalers and AI labs.

Two Harvard Business School cases put the accounting questions on the table:
• 126-034 Meta: Accounting for AI Data Center Depreciation – useful-life extension cut 2025 depreciation by $2.9 billion.
• 126-070 Meta Platforms: Accounting for the AI Arms Race – $27 billion off-balance-sheet Hyperion project with residual-value guarantee.

Future CFOs will not merely report the numbers. They will explain the assumptions, stress-test the shortfall scenarios and keep the statements, footnotes and narrative consistent.
