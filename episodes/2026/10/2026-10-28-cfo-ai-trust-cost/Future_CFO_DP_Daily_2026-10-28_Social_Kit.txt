Post:
The dual mandate has arrived for CFOs.

Deloitte’s latest surveys (Finance Trends 2027 and Q3 2026 CFO Signals) show that 48% of finance leaders now own AI trust and 48% own AI cost controls. More than two-thirds took these responsibilities in the past three years. 54% lead AI capital allocation.

Yet the gap is real: 96% are confident in their governance framework, but 43% report insufficient visibility into AI use and 46% cite cost uncertainty as their biggest concern. Only 20% use advanced AI-specific cost controls.

Organisations with strong cost visibility are 5x more likely to report established ROI.

The next test is not productivity. It is cash flow and decision quality. Great CFOs do not accept AI outputs. They challenge them.
