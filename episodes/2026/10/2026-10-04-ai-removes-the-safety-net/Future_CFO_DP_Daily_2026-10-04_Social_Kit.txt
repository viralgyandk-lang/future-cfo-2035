Hook: AI does not weaken the CFO. It removes the safety net.

That line is from Arvind Kumar Talan, CFO of Energy Efficiency Services Limited, in a BW CFO World conversation dated 19 January 2026 (Savi Khanna). The page is in front of students again on 4 October 2026.

He is specific about what is being commoditised: transaction processing, reconciliations, statutory compliance, even baseline forecasting. Those jobs once conferred authority because they were complex and opaque. That moat is going.

He does not say throw out the quarter. He says keep it for governance, and stop managing only by snapshot. He does not say take every risk. He says price it, stage it, monitor it, and do not waive liquidity or solvency.

The uncomfortable truth, in his words: many finance transformations are automation exercises, not reinvention. Faster execution is not automatically a better outcome. Failure sits in people, incentives, and change fatigue more often than in the tool.

PTI reported his appointment as EESL CFO on 21 June 2024. No internal AI budget is in the interview. We do not invent one.

Three sentences for Monday: what matters, what can wait, what must change.

AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgement decides.
