=== LINKEDIN ===
Hook: Finance teams are spending more than a full day every week fact-checking the AI they adopted to save time.

Post:
The Datarails 2026 CFO Sentiments Survey (released 6 October) shows finance teams spend 26% of their week verifying or correcting AI outputs. 96% of CFOs spend at least 10% of their time on it. Only 5% trust AI for board-ready reports. Only 4% trust it with the close.

Yet only 3% are cutting headcount because of AI. 60% are redeploying people to higher-value work.

Meanwhile, IBM research links AI-first CFOs to 23% higher revenue growth, and 62% of CFOs say their role has already expanded into AI strategy leadership.

The new archetype is the finance engineer — the professional who blends financial expertise with the ability to design data flows, controls, and decision workflows.

AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.

What are you teaching the next generation of finance leaders about verification and judgment?
