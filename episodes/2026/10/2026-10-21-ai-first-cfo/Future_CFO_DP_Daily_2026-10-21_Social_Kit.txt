Title: The AI-First CFO in 2026: 23% Higher Growth, Verification Burden & Human Judgment | Future CFO_DP
Description:
Can AI keep its time-saving promise to CFOs? On 21 October 2026 we examine the latest evidence.

IBM’s 2026 CFO Study (with Oxford Economics): organisations led by AI-first CFOs achieved 23% higher revenue growth. 80% co-architect business models, 84% track AI value and reallocate capital, 90% allow AI in M&A within guardrails — yet only 6% are transformation-ready.

Datarails 2026: 96% of CFOs spend ≥10% of their day verifying AI outputs. Only 4% have a single source of truth. 60% redeploy staff rather than cut headcount.

Andreessen Horowitz: Anthropic’s finance team built 70+ AI skills; one produces a 90-95% complete monthly review in ~30 minutes. OpenAI’s Sarah Friar aims for real-time finance and zero-day close.

Deloitte: 87% see AI as critical by 2026; only 63% deployed. Finance expands into Finance for Finance, Enterprise and Market.

Indian signal: BW CFO World reports up to 10x productivity; great CFOs challenge AI outputs.

Philosophy: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
