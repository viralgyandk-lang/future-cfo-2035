Post:
The numbers from late 2026 are clear. Bain finds 77% of CFOs report unexpected agentic AI costs, yet only 17% are highly concerned about controlling them. Visibility is even weaker: only 10% capture nearly all related spending in formal reporting, and only 8% are very confident they can see every agentic AI tool in use.

Gartner projects $2.52 trillion in global AI spending this year. Skills gaps remain a top barrier for 68% of CFOs.

For business students preparing to become future CFOs, the lesson is practical. Speed without visibility creates leakage. Automation without audit trails creates risk. Human judgment is not optional.
