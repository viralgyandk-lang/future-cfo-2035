YouTube Description:
In this episode of Future CFO_DP we examine how CFOs are expanding their mandate to orchestrate enterprise AI while managing rising costs and complexity. Drawing on the Deloitte Finance Trends 2027 report (September 2026), we cover top priorities, new responsibilities outside traditional finance, stage-gate discipline, real-world signals from Meta, Alphabet and Indian companies, the hidden workload of AI, and why human judgment remains decisive.

Key takeaways:
• 54% of finance leaders lead cross-enterprise AI capital allocation
• 60% expect AI costs to rise substantially through 2027
• Stage-gates and measurement separate builders from scorekeepers
• AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
