Hook: The pure number of “return in how many days” is dead for AI investments.

Post:
Yesterday in Mumbai, CFOs from India’s largest companies agreed on an uncomfortable truth. Girish Goenka, CFO of Piramal Realty, said it plainly: it is no longer a matter of return. It is a matter of existence.

If your competitors’ computers are doing the work and yours are not, you are out of the game.

The new scorecard has three parts:
• Efficiency (process time and error rates)
• Growth (customer outcomes and fewer exceptions)
• Strategy (decision speed and competitive position)

Evidence is already in: HPE cut reporting cycles ~40% with agentic AI. McKinsey reports 65% of CFOs increasing gen-AI spend. Swiggy and Happilo are compressing pipelines and automating order entry.

But speed without redesign just moves the bottleneck. Human judgment still decides.
