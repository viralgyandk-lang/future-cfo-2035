Hook: 86% of UK financial firms now use AI. Only 2% let agents act with broad autonomy. Here is the 2026 reality for future CFOs.

Post:
The Bank of England and FCA just released their fourth AI survey. 86% of respondents use AI. Generative AI is 56% of systems. 80% have agents deployed — mostly for decision support.

US banks are publishing hard numbers: Goldman’s AI assistant reaches all 46,000 employees. Morgan Stanley answers research queries in a tenth of the time. A Bank of America banker can cover 50 clients instead of 15.

Junior analyst seats are narrowing. Goldman’s 2026 summer class was about 100 smaller. The production work that used to fill nights is now drafted in seconds.

Three implications for business students:
1. Master the judgment layer — verify, measure, decide.
2. Work with agents that stay on a short leash.
3. Build domain depth alongside technical fluency.
