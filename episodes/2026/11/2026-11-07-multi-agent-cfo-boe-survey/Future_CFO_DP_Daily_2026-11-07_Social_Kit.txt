On 26 October 2026 the Bank of England and FCA published their fourth AI survey of UK financial services. 86% of firms now use AI. 80% have agents deployed. Only 2% allow broad autonomy.

In this episode we break down the numbers, the third-party concentration risk (top 3 clouds = 79%), and what it means for the CFO of 2030.
