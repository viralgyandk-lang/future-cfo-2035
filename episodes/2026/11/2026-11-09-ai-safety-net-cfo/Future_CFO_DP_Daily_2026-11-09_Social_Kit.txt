Hook: AI does not weaken the CFO. It removes the safety net.

At a BW Businessworld–SAP Concur roundtable this year, CFOs from Swiggy, Randstad India, Happilo, Atyati, EY GDS, EESL and Philips India described what is already happening:

• Swiggy’s engineering team is shipping ~70% more releases. The CFO who never wrote SQL is now vibe coding.
• Randstad cut payroll processing from two weeks to two days across 75,000 workers.
• Atyati’s CFO warned of hallucination in tax work: “We have to be really, really careful.”
• Arvind Kumar Talan (EESL): those who keep defining value through ownership of routines will feel vulnerable.
• Dev Tripathy (Philips India): great CFOs will challenge AI outputs, not accept them.

Four things they agreed on:
1. AI is not one thing.
2. Finance teams are being restructured.
3. Caution is not scepticism.
4. Jobs are changing, not disappearing.

Philosophy we teach: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.

Will you own the interpretation, or will you be left owning the routines that no longer matter?
