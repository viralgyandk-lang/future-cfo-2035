Body:
Anthropic’s record IPO closed this week — roughly $93.5 billion in public proceeds plus a $26 billion strategic private placement. Shares closed day one up 17% at $97.17 under ticker ANT.

Yet Bloomberg reports CFOs are already asking a harder question: where should AI budgets actually go? Not every rupee (or dollar) belongs in finance. McKinsey data shows 65% of CFOs invested in AI in 2025 (up from 24% in 2023), with active use jumping to 51%. Still, more than half of planning time is wasted on unread reports.

IBM finds 60%+ of CFOs have expanded into enterprise AI strategy. Deloitte notes 60% expect AI costs to rise sharply through 2027. The common thread? 97% of finance leaders demand human oversight because most teams have already seen hallucinated data.

Philosophy remains: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
