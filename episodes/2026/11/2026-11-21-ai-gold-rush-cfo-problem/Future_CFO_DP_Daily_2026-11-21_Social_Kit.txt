Post:
The AI gold rush has a CFO problem.

At the Finance and Accounting Technology Expo this month, Glenn Hopper (former CFO, author of The AI-Ready CFO) reacted to a startup’s claim of building a full AI-native ERP in 18 months: “If I’m a CFO and I hear that, I’m nervous.”

The deeper risk is the talent pipeline. Automating away the entry-level roles that teach reconciliation, controls, and the language of the general ledger leaves us without the next generation of controllers and CFOs.

Framework for every future finance leader:
AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.

Existing finance controls are not obstacles to AI agents — they are the framework that makes agents safe. Treat every AI initiative as its own project with its own baseline and kill criteria.

For business students: master the fundamentals. Learn systems thinking. Practice the second and third questions. The tools will change. The need for rigorous human judgment will not.
