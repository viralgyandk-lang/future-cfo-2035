Description:
In late 2026, agentic AI is moving from pilot to production inside finance. HPE’s Alfred, Alphabet’s invoice agents, Trintech’s close agents, and Deloitte’s cost surveys all point to the same reality: the CFO role is expanding from numbers stewardship to intelligence stewardship.

Philips India CFO Dev Tripathy puts it cleanly: great CFOs do not accept AI outputs — they challenge them. By 2030 the operational work will be largely automated. Leadership, strategic clarity and the ability to turn insight into action will separate the best from the rest.

This episode covers:
• Agentic AI in the financial close and budget cycle
• Rising AI costs through 2027 and the expanded CFO mandate
• Controls, rogue-agent risk and audit evidence shifts
• Real-time continuous finance versus the monthly scramble
• Why fractional CFOs are rising with AI force-multipliers
• Three practical implications for business students

Philosophy: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
