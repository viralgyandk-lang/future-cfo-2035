Post:
The AI-first CFO is no longer a future concept.

IBM’s 2026 study of 1,500 CFOs found that organizations led by AI-first CFOs achieved 23% higher revenue growth between 2022 and 2024. These leaders co-architect business models, track AI value continuously, and allow AI a role in capital decisions—within clear guardrails.

Yet only 6% of finance organizations are transformation-ready. Finance is building AI capabilities faster than it is redesigning the work.

Deloitte shows 54% of finance leaders now lead AI capital allocation, while 60% expect AI costs to rise through 2027. McKinsey finds widespread adoption but only 37% reporting positive EBIT impact.

The differentiator is not the model. It is the judgment that challenges the model, sets measurable targets, and redesigns processes end-to-end.

AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
