Post:
77% of CFOs have already seen unexpected agentic AI costs. Only 17% are very concerned about controlling them.

Bain & Company (23 November 2026) surveyed 150 CFOs at companies >$1B revenue. Visibility is weak: only 10% capture nearly all related spend in formal reporting. Only 8% are very confident they see all usage, including shadow AI.

Meanwhile, IBM’s 2026 CFO Study shows organisations led by AI-first CFOs achieved 23% higher revenue growth (2022–2024). The gap is between capability and control.

Future CFOs must own real-time guardrails, AI unit economics, and the judgment to challenge model outputs — not just produce numbers.
