Post:
A Deloitte survey of 1,434 finance leaders at companies with ≥$1B revenue across 26 countries found that 60% expect AI costs and technical complexity to rise substantially through 2027. Fifty-four percent are already leading enterprise AI capital-allocation decisions. Forty-eight percent oversee spending and cost controls.

The practical response is stage-gate funding: pilot first, release further capital only when measurable gates are met. In India, CFOs are already seeing productivity compression—engineering teams shipping ~70% more releases, finance teams building outputs that once required multiple specialists. Middle-market firms report that 70% redeploy talent rather than reduce headcount.

Agentic finance is coming. Most organizations are not yet ready. Cost discipline is the new competitive advantage.
