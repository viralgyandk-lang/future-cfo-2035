Post:
The AI cost reckoning is here.

Deloitte’s latest research shows 60% of finance leaders expect AI costs and technical complexity to rise substantially through 2027. Only 35% believe costs will remain modest. The challenges? Uncertain regulation, complex cloud and vendor billing, and difficulty integrating AI usage data with ERP systems.

At the same time, IBM’s 2026 CFO Study of 1,500 finance chiefs finds that 62% have seen their responsibilities expand into enterprise technology and AI strategy leadership in recent years. Organizations led by “AI-first CFOs” delivered 23% higher revenue growth from 2022–2024.

Finance is no longer just reporting the past. It is allocating capital, governing AI trust, and measuring whether the technology actually creates value.

For business students and aspiring CFOs: the skill that matters most is not coding models. It is asking the right questions about cost, ROI, risk, and accountability.
