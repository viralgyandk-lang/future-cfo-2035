Title: 83% of APAC CFOs Say AI Is Reshaping Finance | Future CFO_DP Daily 2 Nov 2026
Description:
Eighty-three percent of chief financial officers across the Asia-Pacific say artificial intelligence is reshaping the finance function. The finding comes from the Wolters Kluwer Future Ready CFO report (survey of 1,672 senior finance leaders, 484 from APAC). Seventy-two percent expect significant operational impact within three years in FP&A, forecasting, risk, and compliance.

Yet the same leaders insist on governance, clear ROI, regulatory compliance, and human oversight. Philips India CFO Dev Tripathy puts it plainly: great CFOs will challenge AI outputs, not simply accept them.

In this episode we examine the data, the strategic shift, and the preparation business students need today.

Philosophy: AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
