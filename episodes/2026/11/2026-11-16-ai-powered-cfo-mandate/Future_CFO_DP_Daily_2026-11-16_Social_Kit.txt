=== LINKEDIN ===
Hook: More than 6 in 10 CFOs now own AI strategy. Most finance teams are not ready.

Post:
The 2026 data are unambiguous.

IBM: >60% of CFOs expanded into enterprise technology and AI strategy. AI-first CFOs linked to 23% higher revenue growth 2022–2024. Only 6% of finance organizations are transformation-ready.

Deloitte: 54% of finance leaders lead AI capital allocation. 60% expect costs to rise substantially through 2027.

FERF: 53% plan significant AI tech spend. Only 3% have AI as a core integrated function. In 36% of organizations senior staff still spend 31–50% of their time as “human middleware.”

At the FICCI CFO Summit, Reliance Jio CFO Rajneesh Jain drew the line clearly: the goal is not AI-driven finance. It is intelligent finance where human judgment is amplified, not replaced.

For business students the implication is practical. Technical fluency is necessary. Measurement, verification, and judgment remain the differentiators.

AI generates. Data informs. Finance measures. Accounting verifies. Human judgment decides.
