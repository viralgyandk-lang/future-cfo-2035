Description:
AI does not weaken the CFO—it removes the safety net. Deloitte’s Finance Trends 2027 survey of 1,434 finance leaders shows 54% now lead cross-enterprise AI capital allocation and 48% oversee AI trust and spending. Indian CFOs including Arvind Kumar Talan (EESL) and Rajneesh Jain (Reliance Jio) explain why interpretation, capital stewardship and human judgment will define the role by 2030.

Key points from public sources:
• 43% prioritise embedding AI to automate operations
• Jobs changing, not disappearing — upskilling is core
• 1 in 3 finance job postings now require AI skills on top of GAAP/forecasting
• 60% expect AI costs to rise substantially through 2027

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
