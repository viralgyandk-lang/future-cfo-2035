Description:
On 11 November 2026, we examine how CFOs are redesigning the finance function around agentic AI while protecting human judgment.

Key public facts:
• Deloitte Finance Trends 2027: 54% of finance leaders lead AI/tech capital allocation; 60% expect AI costs to rise substantially through 2027; only 14% comfortable with fully autonomous agents on critical decisions.
• IBM: >60% of CFOs expanded into AI strategy; AI-first CFOs saw +23% revenue growth (2022-2024); only 6% transformation-ready.
• BDO Audit Innovation Survey (4 Nov 2026): 99% use AI for financial estimates reviewed by auditors; only 49% have full documentation.
• AICPA issued 51-page practice aid on AI in auditing (Oct 2026).
• Philips India CFO: Great CFOs challenge AI outputs.

Teaching case: Nexus Dynamics Inc. – CFO Priya Mehta chooses among Efficiency, Intelligence Platform, or Selective Agentic + Controls.

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
