YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION
Yesterday Wolters Kluwer released the third edition of its Future Ready Accountant report. Based on more than 2,600 accounting professionals worldwide, the findings show a profession that is growing and confident, yet still operating largely task by task.

Key numbers:
- 85% report increased revenue, 81% improved profitability
- 70% say AI delivered better-than-expected results in client advisory
- Only 11% have at least 75% of technology solutions integrated
- 95% of firms with formal AI governance are optimistic about the future of the profession versus 77% with none

This episode examines what the data means for students and early-career professionals preparing for the AI-powered economy. Full case study, slides, and script available.
