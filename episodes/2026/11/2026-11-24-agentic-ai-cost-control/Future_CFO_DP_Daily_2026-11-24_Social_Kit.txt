77% of CFOs report unexpected agentic AI costs. Only 17% are highly concerned.

Bain & Company surveyed 150 senior finance leaders (companies >$1B revenue). 97% expect spending to rise; 66% expect at least 25% growth. Only 10% of those deploying agents capture nearly all related costs in formal reporting.

In India, Avalara found 85% of CFOs under pressure to show AI value, while 71% say the focus is speed of deployment, not governance.

For the future CFO: AI generates the output. Finance must measure the full cost. Accounting must verify. Human judgment must decide.
