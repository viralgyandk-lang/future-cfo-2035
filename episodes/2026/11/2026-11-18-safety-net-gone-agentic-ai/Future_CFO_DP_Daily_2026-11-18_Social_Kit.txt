Hook: The safety net is gone.

For years CFOs could hide behind the complexity of the close. Agentic AI is stripping that buffer away. Trintech’s Omar Choucair put it plainly: the last thing any CFO wants is a rogue AI agent inside the record-to-report process. Time saved by agents must be spent reviewing what the agents actually did.

Fresh data confirms the tension. Rightworks (Nov 2026): 71% of accounting firms are using or testing AI — a 78% surge from 2024. Active users report 39% higher revenue per employee. Yet 63% of those firms have no formal AI policy. Privacy and accuracy have replaced knowledge as the top concerns.

BearingPoint finds 75% of CFOs expect high impact by 2030 while 74% still describe current adoption as minimal or basic. Skills gaps are the largest barrier to ROI. IBM shows more than 60% of CFOs have already expanded into AI strategy.

The future CFO owns three things: the controls, the talent, and the measurement.
