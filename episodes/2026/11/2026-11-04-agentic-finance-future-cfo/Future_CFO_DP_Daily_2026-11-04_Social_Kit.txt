Post:
The Wolters Kluwer Future Ready CFO 2026 report, based on 1,672 senior finance leaders, shows 83% of APAC CFOs view AI adoption as a key force reshaping finance. 72% expect significant operational impact within three years, especially in FP&A, forecasting, and risk management.

Meanwhile, the OneStream Forward Finance Index of 1,300 CFOs finds only 6% qualify as true leaders ready for Agentic Finance—where AI agents reason, plan, and act within defined controls.

Barriers remain clear: cost versus expected return (58%), concerns over loss of human judgment (55%), and data quality/governance (53%).

For business students, the message is direct. Technical fluency is table stakes. The differentiators will be the ability to challenge AI outputs, design governance frameworks, and exercise professional judgment.
