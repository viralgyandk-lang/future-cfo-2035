Six days to the 57th GST Council.

Public facts, not rumours:
• Meeting: New Delhi, 12 September 2026, 11:00. Officers meet on 11 September. First Council sitting after 374 days.
• On the public radar: inverted duty; possible GST cut on mobile handsets from 18%; registration for large businesses passing ITC above Rs 2.5 lakh a month.
• Live since 1 April 2026: zero-mismatch. If GSTR-2B and claimed ITC differ, the return does not file.
• 4 September: ICAI released the 14th edition of Background Material on GST, updated for Finance Act 2026 and GST 2.0.
• Advisers quoted in the business press put leftover compensation-cess credit at a conservative Rs 6,000 crore industry-wide. Provisional refunds from Budget 2026 still exclude input services.

The teaching question: if you are CFO of a labelled composite electronics firm, do you pre-buy inventory on an 18% assumption, hold cash for a possible rate cut, litigate blocked ITC, or wait six days and look unprepared?
