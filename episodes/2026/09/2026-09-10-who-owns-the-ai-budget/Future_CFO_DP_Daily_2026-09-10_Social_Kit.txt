Title: Who Owns the AI Budget? | Future CFO_DP Daily 10 Sep 2026
Description:
On 9 September 2026, Deloitte (Finance Trends 2027; 1,434 respondents) and Salesforce (CFO Priorities Report; 865 finance leaders) published separate surveys. Both say the same thing: finance now sits on AI capital allocation.

Verified figures you will hear:
- 54% of finance leaders lead cross-enterprise AI and technology capital allocation (Deloitte).
- 48% oversee AI trust / reliable outputs; 48% control AI and technology spend (Deloitte).
- 95% are comfortable with agentic workflows in at least some finance work; only 14% want full autonomy on critical decisions (Deloitte).
- 55% require human oversight before deploying AI agents in finance (Deloitte).
- 43% prioritize AI and advanced technology through FY2027 (Deloitte).
- 36% see primary AI return as productivity; 23% as revenue enablement (Deloitte).
- Nearly three-quarters of CFOs say the role expanded this year — managing AI is the biggest reason (Salesforce).
- 65% manage multiple revenue models; 71% sell through more channels than a year ago (Salesforce).
- 90% of finance leaders who use AI report ROI; security (46%) and governance/integration (42%) remain the top blockers (Salesforce).

Teaching case company is composite: Deccan Industrial Systems Ltd. Decision-maker is composite: CFO Arjun Menon.

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
