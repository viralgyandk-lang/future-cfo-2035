Title:
Eurofiber €2.2bn SLL: When ESG Moves the Coupon | Future CFO_DP

Description:
Sustainability-linked loans change the interest rate when ESG targets are missed. On 25 August 2026 Eurofiber refinanced €1.5bn into a €2.2bn Sustainability-Linked Loan tied to SBTi emissions, circularity, and gender diversity.

This Future CFO_DP Daily case walks finance students through the difference between a green loan and an SLL, the LMA Sustainability-Linked Loan Principles, margin-adjustment mechanics, and the CFO decision: how tight should the KPI ratchet be when undrawn facilities fund European fibre and cloud expansion?
