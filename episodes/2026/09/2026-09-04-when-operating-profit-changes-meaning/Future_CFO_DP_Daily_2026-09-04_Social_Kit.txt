Title:
IFRS 18 / Ind AS 118: When Operating Profit Changes | Future CFO_DP
Description:
IFRS 18 and the proposed Ind AS 118 will restyle the P&L for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027 globally and, on NFRA’s recommendation, 1 April 2027 for most Indian companies.

This Future CFO_DP Daily teaching case does not invent rupees for a listed ticker. It uses a labelled composite — Aarohan Industrials and CFO Meera Iyer — to force a real decision: wait for the MCA Gazette, run a shadow P&L, early-apply only if notified, or retire “core operating EBITDA” before it becomes a management-defined performance measure.

IASB issued IFRS 18 on 9 April 2024, replacing IAS 1. Three categories (operating, investing, financing). Two required subtotals (operating profit; profit before financing and income tax). Public subtotals of income and expense become MPMs with a tax and NCI bridge.

ICAI issued the Ind AS 118 exposure draft on 6 January 2025. NFRA’s 47th meeting (22 December 2025) recommended adoption from 1 April 2027, with calendar-year early application from 1 January 2027. MCA notification and the Schedule III rewrite remained pending on the date of this episode. KPMG India’s June 2026 Accounting and Auditing Update already treats the standard as a live board item.

Hook:
Operating profit is about to stop meaning whatever last year’s investor deck said it meant.
Body:
On 9 April 2024 the IASB issued IFRS 18. It replaces IAS 1 from periods beginning 1 January 2027. India is on a parallel track: ICAI’s Ind AS 118 exposure draft (6 January 2025) and NFRA’s 22 December 2025 recommendation of a 1 April 2027 date for March-year-end companies.

This is not a new measurement model. It is a new grouping discipline — operating / investing / financing — plus two mandatory subtotals, plus a note for every public income-and-expense subtotal that management still uses outside the statements.

Today’s Future CFO_DP Daily case refuses to invent rupees for a live ticker. The composite CFO still has to choose: wait for the Gazette, dual-run a shadow P&L, early-apply only if notified, or retire the homemade EBITDA.

AI can draft the mapping memo. The general ledger has to feed it. Human judgment decides which number you will defend when operating profit moves and cash does not.
