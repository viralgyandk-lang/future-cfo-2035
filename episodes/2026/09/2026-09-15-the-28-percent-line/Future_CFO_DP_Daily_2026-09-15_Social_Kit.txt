Post:
On 10 September 2026, CFO Dive reported a PEX finding: only 28% of finance teams are comfortable letting AI decide routine finance decisions.
That number is not anti-technology. It is a control design number.
On the same news cycle, Deloitte’s CFO survey said nearly two-thirds expect AI costs and operational complexity to rise through 2027 — and many will still fund the stack.
And on 14 September, Hub Group named a CFO-elect while restating three years after a $77 million accounting error. Restatements are what happen when verification fails, whether the hand on the keyboard is human or machine.
Question for B.Com / MBA / CA students:
If you were CFO for one week, which class of “routine” journals would you allow an agent to propose — and which class would still require a named human signature?
