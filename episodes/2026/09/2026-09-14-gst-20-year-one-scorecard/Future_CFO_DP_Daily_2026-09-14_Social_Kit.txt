Description:
One year after GST 2.0 took effect on 22 September 2025, the 57th GST Council meeting that was expected around 12 September 2026 has been rescheduled to 7 October 2026 in New Delhi. The two-rate structure (5% merit, 18% standard, 40% demerit) is no longer a launch story. It is a working-capital story.

This briefing uses only public facts:
• 56th GST Council (3–4 September 2025) approved GST 2.0.
• CBIC notifications dated 17 September 2025; rates effective 22 September 2025.
• RBI MPC 3–5 August 2026 held repo at 5.25% (neutral stance). FY27 CPI projection 5.0%.
• SBI Research EcoWrap (reported 11 September 2026) flags a possible 25 bps hike in October and another in December if crude stays above $100/bbl.
• Business Today (8 September 2026): Council likely to take stock of one-year rationalisation; broad-based cuts unlikely.
