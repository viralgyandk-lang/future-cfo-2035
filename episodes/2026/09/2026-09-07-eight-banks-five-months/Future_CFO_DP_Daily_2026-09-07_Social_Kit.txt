Title: Eight Banks, Five Months — RBI Just Rewrote Who Can Be a Bank CFO (7 Sep 2026)
Description:
In about five months of mid-2026, at least eight systemically important Indian banks changed a senior finance or governance officer. That is not a coincidence. It is what happens when a circular becomes a labour market.

On 28 November 2025 the Reserve Bank issued the Commercial Banks – Governance Directions, 2025. Paragraph 36: a bank CFO must be a qualified Chartered Accountant with fifteen years overseeing financial operations — ten of those in banks or FIs, five at senior management. Those minima now apply to private banks as well as public-sector banks.

This episode is an HBS-style case. Sutlej Bank’s NRC has two files. One internal controller is a year short. One portable external CV clears the rule in a week. Who sits?
