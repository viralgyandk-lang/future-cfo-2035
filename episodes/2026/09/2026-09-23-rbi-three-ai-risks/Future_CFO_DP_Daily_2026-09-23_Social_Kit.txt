Hook: RBI did not ban AI in finance. It named the three ways AI can bankrupt a good process.
Body:
Deputy Governor Rohit Jain, GFF 2026 (as reported 9 Sep 2026): speed, concentration, opacity.

Speed: the model declines a borrower before collections even sees the file.
Concentration: three vendors sit under your GL. One outage is an operations event. Two is a going-concern footnote.
Opacity: if you cannot explain the score to the audit committee, you do not own the risk. The vendor does.

Student drill: pick one automated action in a credit shop. Write the human gate. Write the kill switch. Write who signs.
