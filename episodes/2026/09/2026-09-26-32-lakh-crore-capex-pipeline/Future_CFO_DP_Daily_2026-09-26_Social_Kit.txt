Post:
Yesterday the RBI Bulletin (25 September 2026) reported Q1 FY27 growth of 7.8 percent, August headline CPI of 4.8 percent, foreign-exchange reserves at an all-time high, and a liquidity surplus after FCNR(B) deposit flows.

The same Bulletin estimates envisaged private corporate capex of Rs 3.2 lakh crore in 2026-27 from the existing project pipeline.

That is a national number. Your board will still ask three local questions:
1. What is our hurdle after tax, after risk, after working-capital drag?
2. Which projects are demand-backed versus hope-backed?
3. Who signs the forecast if energy prices or food inflation move again?

Repo is unchanged at 5.25 percent since the August MPC. Cheap headline money is not the same as a cheap project.
