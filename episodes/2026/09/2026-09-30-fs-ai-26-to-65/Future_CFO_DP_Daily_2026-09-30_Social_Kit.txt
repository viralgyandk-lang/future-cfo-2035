LINKEDIN
26 percent to 65 percent.
That is not a pilot. That is an operating-system change.

KPMG in India and Mastercard, presenting at Global Fintech Fest 2026, say AI adoption across Indian financial services is expected to rise from 26% to 65% within twelve months. India now counts more than 17,700 fintech companies. Roughly 2.9 billion financial accounts can share data through the Account Aggregator framework. More than 538 million consents had already been fulfilled by July 2026.

CFA Institute’s 2026 Graduate Outlook adds the labour-market sting: 74% of Indian graduates think AI could make jobs harder to get, even though 81% feel confident using the tools. Ninety-eight percent say upskilling is essential. Nearly 70% now rate professional certifications above a postgraduate degree. Randstad Digital reports that more than 30% of organisations in India plan to cut graduate hiring as AI scales.

If you are a commerce student, do not memorise the headline. Ask the CFO question:
1. Which process is data-rich enough to scale this year?
2. Who owns model risk when a credit score moves?
3. Where does a human still have to sign?
