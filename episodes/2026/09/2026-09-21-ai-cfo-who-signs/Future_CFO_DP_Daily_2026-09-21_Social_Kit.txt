In the second half of September 2026, three stories landed on the same desk.

Deloitte told 1,434 finance leaders across 26 countries that 60 percent expect AI costs and technical complexity to rise substantially through 2027 — and 54 percent of those leaders already lead enterprise AI and technology capital allocation.

Runway Financial rebranded as cfo.ai and launched Ari, an always-on agent that connects to NetSuite, QuickBooks, Stripe, Salesforce and Excel, then forecasts cash, tests hiring plans and pokes founders in Slack.

FICCI CFO Summit in Hyderabad asked Indian finance leaders to reimagine the finance role in the age of AI. Microsoft India CFO Aruna Singh and Google India Head of Finance Vivek Chhabra both put data foundations ahead of dashboards.

This episode of Future CFO_DP is a teaching case, not a product review. The question is not whether an agent can draft a forecast. The question is who signs it.
