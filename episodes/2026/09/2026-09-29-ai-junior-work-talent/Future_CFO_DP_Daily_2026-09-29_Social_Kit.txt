Description:
CFO.com asked the question this week: if AI absorbs the junior tasks that used to train finance people, where does judgment come from?

Public facts in this episode:
- Teikametrics CFO Brian Beaupre is changing how he evaluates young candidates (CFO.com, Adam Zaki, 25 Sep 2026).
- Meta CAO Aaron Anderson: AI flux analysis cut Meta close from seven days to six; about 550 staff complete AI fluency plus three days in person (CFO.com, 28 Sep 2026).
- KPMG Global AI in Finance 2026: 75% of finance executives say their organisations actively use AI (up from 30% in 2024).
- Grant Thornton Q2 2026 CFO survey: 97% piloting, scaling, or fully integrating AI.
- Revelio Labs: U.S. entry-level postings down about 35% since Jan 2023.
- AICPA / National Student Clearinghouse: U.S. accounting enrollment +7.3% YoY.

Philosophy: AI generates. DATA informs. FINANCE measures. ACCOUNTING verifies. HUMAN JUDGMENT decides.
