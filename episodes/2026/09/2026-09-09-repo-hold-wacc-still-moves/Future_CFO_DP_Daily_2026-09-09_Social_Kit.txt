Hook: The repo did not move. Your WACC still might.
Body:
On 3–5 August 2026 the RBI MPC held the policy repo at 5.25%, kept a neutral stance, and published a 2026-27 path of 6.7% real GDP and 5.0% CPI — with Q3 inflation projected at 5.9%.
A hold is a fact about the overnight corridor. It is not a fact about a mid-cap’s floating cash-credit book, the inflation block inside cost of equity, or a term loan that expires on 31 December.
Today’s Future CFO_DP case puts a composite CFO, Meera Kulkarni of Godavari Precision Components, in front of a Friday board pack. Three questions. No slogans.
If you teach or study finance, separate three columns before you argue: corridor, path, firm.
