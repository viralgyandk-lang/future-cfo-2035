Post:
On 18 September 2026, CEA V. Anantha Nageswaran spoke at the 16th Indian Management Conclave.

His line will travel farther than any new elective catalogue:

“The trouble is AI now precisely does this junior work. The bottom rungs of the ladder are being sawn off.”

For a century, finance talent learned on variance analysis, reconciliations, first-pass models, and the Friday night pack. That work was never glamorous. It was the apprenticeship.

If software now writes the first pack, the scarce skill is not another prompt. It is knowing which question to ask, and whether the number that comes back can be signed.

AIMS–EPSI–IMC scored the Top 75 B-schools at 3.2/5 against an industry bar of 4/5.

Future CFOs will not be hired for technique the model already has. They will be hired for judgement the model cannot own.
