Title:
GST 2.0 Working Capital Test | Repo Held at 5.25% | Future CFO_DP
Description:
One year after GST 2.0 went live on 22 September 2025, the tax cut is no longer a press release. It is a working-capital problem.

This Future CFO_DP Daily teaching case does not invent rupees for a listed ticker. It uses a labelled composite — Navtara Consumer Products and CFO Arjun Sen — against two public facts: the 56th GST Council two-rate structure (5% merit, 18% standard, 40% demerit) and the RBI MPC decision of 3-5 August 2026 to hold the policy repo at 5.25 per cent with a neutral stance.

RBI August 2026 statement projected real GDP for 2026-27 at 6.7 per cent and CPI at 5.0 per cent. Q1:2026-27 GST e-way bills rose 12.4 per cent; GST revenue rose 8.6 per cent. Government estimated annualised fiscal impact about Rs 48,000 crore. SBI Research (4 September 2025) argued CPI could ease 65-75 basis points over FY26-27 if pass-through holds.
